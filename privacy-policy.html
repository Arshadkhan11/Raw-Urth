Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 July 2026

Raw Urth Consultancy ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy and is committed to handling data responsibly. This policy explains how we collect, use and protect information — both from visitors to this website and within the databases we provide to our clients.

1. Information We Collect

When you contact us, request a quote, or make a payment, we may collect: your name, email address, phone number, company name, and any details you share about your requirement. When you make a payment, transaction details are processed securely by our payment partner, Razorpay — we do not store your card, UPI or bank details on our own systems.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to enquiries and prepare quotes
  • To deliver the databases or services you've purchased
  • To process payments and issue receipts/invoices
  • To maintain records required for accounting and legal compliance
  • To follow up on ongoing or future engagements

3. Our Data Sourcing Commitment

The candidate and business databases we provide to clients are compiled with appropriate rights and consent for their intended business use. We take reasonable steps to ensure the data we collect and share is handled responsibly and in line with applicable Indian data protection laws, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, where applicable.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. Client and website-visitor data is shared only with trusted service providers where necessary to operate our business — for example, our payment processor (Razorpay) for handling transactions, or cloud/email providers for communication and delivery.

5. Data Security

We use secure, access-controlled channels to deliver data and store client information. While we take reasonable precautions to protect information, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information held by us by writing to help@rawurth.in. If your details appear in a database sourced through us and you wish to be removed, please contact us with relevant details and we will act on genuine requests.

7. Cookies

This website does not use tracking cookies beyond what is strictly necessary for it to function.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision.
